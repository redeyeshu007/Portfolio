# From College Student to Full-Stack Developer

I started where most do: a college course, a confusing IDE, and a "Hello World" that took an hour to run. This is the path from there to shipping complete, real-world products.

## The roadmap that worked

1. **Fundamentals first** — one language deeply, not five shallowly.
2. **Frontend** — HTML, CSS, then React to make ideas visible.
3. **Backend** — Node.js, APIs, and how data actually flows.
4. **Databases** — modeling data, not just querying it.
5. **Glue skills** — Git, Docker, deployment, debugging in production.

## Mistakes I made

- **Tutorial hell.** I watched far more than I built, for far too long.
- **Chasing every new framework** instead of going deep on fundamentals.
- **Avoiding hard problems** because they were uncomfortable — which is exactly why they were worth doing.

## What changed everything

Building real projects and entering hackathons forced me to *finish* things. Shipping — not studying — is what turned me from a student into a developer.

If you're on this path: build more than you watch, finish what you start, and don't wait until you "feel ready." You won't. Build anyway.
