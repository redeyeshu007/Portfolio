# My Journey Through 10+ Competitions: Wins, Failures & Lessons

Ten-plus competitions later, I've shipped working products under tight deadlines and I've also watched demos crash in front of judges. Both taught me things no classroom did.

## What winning teams do differently

- **Scope ruthlessly.** The team that builds *one* thing well beats the team that half-builds five.
- **Demo early, demo often.** If it isn't demoable by hour 12, it probably won't be by hour 24.
- **Tell a story.** Judges remember the problem you solved, not your tech stack.

## My biggest failures

- Spending the first 6 hours arguing about architecture instead of building.
- Picking an unfamiliar framework "to learn it" — during a timed competition.
- Forgetting that a *working* prototype beats a beautiful broken one.

## The real takeaway

Competitions taught me to make decisions fast, cut scope without guilt, and build under pressure. Those skills carry into every real project I take on — the deadline is just longer.
